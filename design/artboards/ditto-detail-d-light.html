Ditto

Bakumon Wiki · Spawn data from this server's own config

Ditto spawns on Bakumon in two buckets at once, depending on where you are standing.

Ditto is Rare inside slime chunks and Ultra-rare everywhere else. Both rows are under Spawning.

Reading spawn rows

Bucket
How rare the spawn is. Ultra-rare is Bakumon's own tier above rare.
Weight
Chance relative to other spawns in the same bucket and place. Higher is more often.
Level
Level range the Pokemon spawns at.
Context
Where it appears: on the ground, in water, from fishing.
Biomes
Where the row applies. The group icon marks a biome tag covering many biomes.
Conditions
Time, weather, moon, chunk and anything else the row needs.
