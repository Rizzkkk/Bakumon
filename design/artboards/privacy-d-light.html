Privacy

Last updated [DATE]

Short version: this site does not know who you are, and it does not try to find out.

What we collect

Nothing tied to you. There are no accounts, no logins, no comments and no forms. You cannot give us personal data through this site because there is nowhere to put it.

No analytics

We do not use Google Analytics or any other third-party analytics, tracking pixels or advertising scripts. That is a standing decision, not a setting we might flip later.

Server logs

Like most websites, our web server writes a standard access log: IP address, page requested, time and browser type. We use it only to keep the site running and to deal with abuse, and delete it after [RETENTION PERIOD].

Stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark themes, that choice is saved in your own browser. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.

Links out

The Join the Discord button takes you to Discord. Once you are there, Discord's own privacy policy applies.
