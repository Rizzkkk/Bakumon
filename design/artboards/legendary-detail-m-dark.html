Articuno is one of the legendary Pokemon on Bakumon. Like every legendary here, it does not appear in the biome spawn tables. It spawns at random instead.

Spawning

Legendary spawn

Articuno spawns at random.

Legendaries are not part of Bakumon's regular spawn tables. They turn up at random, with no schedule and no player count to wait for, so keep a few Poke Balls on you.
