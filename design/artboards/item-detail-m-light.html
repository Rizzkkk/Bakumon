The Fire Stone is an evolution item. Five Pokemon on Bakumon evolve with it.

Contents
  1. Description
  2. Evolution uses

Description

Makes certain Pokemon evolve when used on them. The stone is used up in the process. It glows warm orange, with a flame-shaped mark at its core.
